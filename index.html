Impression

Record your voice dataset

You'll read sentences out loud, one at a time. 150 is the minimum; finishing all gives your model the best shot. The sentences are ordered so the most useful ones come first.

Takes save in this browser, so you can stop and come back later on the same computer and browser.

Before you start

  • Use a laptop in a quiet room with soft surfaces. A closet full of clothes works great.
  • Keep the same spot, mic, and distance (about a hand's width from your laptop) every session.
  • Read in a calm, natural, even tone. The model copies everything, including your mood.
  • Stumbled? Just press Space and record it again.
  • Do it in 2 or 3 sittings. Your voice drifts when you're tired.

Mic check

Takes about 8 seconds. It measures your room's background noise and how loud your voice comes through.
